The exact strategy (spec v1.2)

Every rule the signal engine applies, verbatim from docs/STRATEGY_SPEC.md. All thresholds are configuration, not code.

1 · Instruments & timeframes

  • Pair: XAUUSD (gold). GBPUSD and EURUSD were tested and removed — both negative out-of-sample (2025+) under every exit model.
  • Entry timeframes: 15m (priority) and 5m, run as separate streams.
  • Analysis stack (top-down): 3-month → Monthly → Weekly → Daily → 3h → 45m → entry timeframe.
  • All day/week/month boundaries use the 5:00pm ET broker day (America/New_York, DST-aware).

2 · Long-term bias (Monthly, Weekly, Daily)

  • On each timeframe: close above the 20-period SMA → bullish; below → bearish.
  • All three must agree — trade only that direction. Any disagreement → no signal (reversals are skipped).

3 · Intermediate confirmation (3h & 45m)

  • Both must be on the correct side of their 20 SMA, and
  • show healthy candles: none of the last 3 closed candles is a momentum candle against the trade direction.

4 · Higher-timeframe levels

  • From the previous completed month and 3-month period: the high, the low, and the 50% midpoint (fixed once the period closes — no repainting).
  • "At a level" = the trigger candle touches it, or closes within 0.25 × ATR(14, daily) of it.

5 · Candle classification

  • Momentum: body ≥ 70% of range — conviction in the close direction.
  • Doji: body ≤ 10% of range — indecision, never a trigger.
  • Rejection: combined wicks > body; the dominant wick names it (long lower wick = bullish rejection).

6 · Session filter

  • Signals only when the trigger candle sits fully inside 8:30am – 12:00pm New York.

7 · Entry (bullish; bearish is the exact mirror)

  1. Monthly, Weekly, Daily all bullish (§2).
  2. 3h and 45m confirm (§3).
  3. Entry-timeframe close above its 20 SMA.
  4. Close above the 5:00pm-ET daily open (a retest that closes back above still qualifies).
  5. Trigger: a bullish momentum or bullish rejection candle forming at a decision point — touching the entry 20 SMA, the daily open, or an HTF level (§4).

8 · Exits & risk

  • Stop loss: 5 pips beyond the most recent confirmed 15m swing point (more extreme than the 2 candles each side, confirmed once those close). Always the 15m swing, even for 5m entries. Gold: 5 pips = $0.50.
  • Take profit (v1.2 default): 3R target with a 1.5R trailing stop that arms once price is +1.5R in favor; the trade ends at stop, trail-out, or 3R. (v1.1 fixed 2R remains available in config.)
  • No time-based exit; one open signal at a time per pair/timeframe.
Simulation rules: spread charged on every entry; a 1-minute candle touching both stop and target counts as a loss; one open signal at a time. Data: Dukascopy 1m bid candles. Session window 08:30–12:00 New York. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
